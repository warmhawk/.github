# Security Policy

WarmHawk runs your mailbox credentials, DNS, and TLS certificates on
infrastructure you own. We take reports about all of that seriously.

This file is the org-wide default (applies to any WarmHawk repo without
its own `SECURITY.md`). Full coordinated-disclosure policy, scope, and
safe-harbor terms: **https://warmhawk.com/security**

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@warmhawk.com** with the affected component, steps to
reproduce, and the impact you believe it has. Non-destructive
proof-of-concept details help us triage faster. Avoid including live
credentials or customer data in the report itself.

## Response time

- First response within **1 business day**
- **4 business hours** for anything critical

We'll keep you updated as triage and a fix progress, and credit your
report (if you'd like) once it's resolved.

## Safe harbor

We won't pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who test
within the published scope, avoid destructive actions, and report
privately before any public disclosure — see the full policy for
details.
